Alessandro Gentili is an Italian author and strategist with a background in strategic marketing, communication and editorial systems. His work connects writing, systems thinking, AI and civic responsibility.

This website is mainly in Italian because the essays depend on language, civic nuance and authorial precision. Selected texts include, or may include, an English note for international readers.

An atlas for reading the present

The present is
not self-evident.

What we accept as normal has a history. So do our desires, our institutions and the words we use to understand them.

This editorial project by Alessandro Gentili brings cultural reading and strategic thinking into the same inquiry.

Judgment / Triad 09

before we choose?

Desire, other people and the framing of a problem help shape the decision we experience as our own.

  1. Desire & freedom

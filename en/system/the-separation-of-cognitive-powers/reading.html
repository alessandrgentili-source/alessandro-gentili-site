Three principles, three checks

Teaching paraphrases of three selected principles from Article 5 of the GDPR.

Quantity → Data minimisation

Limit the data to what is relevant and necessary for the purpose.

Accuracy → Accuracy principle

Check that the data is accurate and, where necessary, up to date.

Duration → Storage limitation

Retention is tied to the purposes of processing. For a specific case, consider the applicable rules and any exceptions.

La decima soglia non racconta una genealogia della comunicazione. Lucrezio interroga la materia e il modo in cui il reale raggiunge i sensi; McLuhan osserva i media come ambienti che riorganizzano percezione, scala e relazioni; Shannon misura ciò che può essere rappresentato e trasmesso attraverso un canale sottoposto a vincoli e rumore. Materia, medium e informazione restano tre livelli irriducibili che il presente tende facilmente a sovrapporre.

Lucretius · Marshall McLuhan · Claude Shannon

What changes when we distinguish what the world is made of, the media through which we encounter it, and what can be transmitted?

The real reaches the senses materially

Lucretius asks what bodies are made of and how images, movements and material processes can make the world perceptible without turning nature into a message addressed to us.

A medium reorganizes the environment of experience

McLuhan asks what media do to perception, scale and relation when technologies become ordinary environments rather than visible tools.

Read McLuhan ↗

Transmission has formal limits

Shannon asks how much information can be represented and transmitted through a channel under constraints and noise without requiring a theory of meaning.

Regulation (EU) 2016/679
Article 5(1)(e)
“for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which the personal data are processed”

Short extract from Article 5(1)(e). The indexed EUR-Lex text was checked on 9 November 2026 for the original presentation. The linked English text provides the full context.

The retention period is tied to the purposes of processing. The provision also allows longer retention for certain purposes, subject to adequate safeguards. It sets no universal 30-day deadline.

This teaching paraphrase does not determine a retention period for an operational case.

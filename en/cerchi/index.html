Cerchi d’inchiostro · Circles of Ink

Authors, ideas and systems for reading the present.

Cerchi d’inchiostro is an editorial project built around authors rather than around summaries. Each guide reconstructs a system of thought, identifies a human problem and asks where that system still exerts pressure on contemporary life.

English rollout

Complete English corpus: eight triads, twenty-four autonomous guides.

The Italian archive contains 24 author guides and eight triads. The English edition now completes the same author-and-triad architecture through edited localizations.

How the project works

A guide is a node, not an endpoint.

Author pages connect biography, works, concepts and present-day tensions. Triads then place three authors around a shared question. Themes gather the questions that recur across different authors. The aim is not to reduce complexity but to make relations navigable.

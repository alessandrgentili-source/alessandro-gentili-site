Knots

Relations · Connections · Filigree

Thought that changes.

Every knot holds an encounter.
Every ray, a way to go further.

The written page fixes thought in one of its possible forms. Knots sets the page in motion again.

The works, authors and essays of Circles of Ink enter a web of relations in which even the ideas we have defended lose their immunity. Distant ages illuminate one another; disciplines foreign to each other meet over the same fragment of reality. At times, what seemed irreconcilable reveals a kinship. At others, a resemblance conceals a fracture.

It is there, in the fractures, that we search.

Thought turns, twists, breaks against the evidence of a fact or the resistance of another interpretation. From the collision may emerge a distinction we had never seen, or the need to relinquish a conviction. Error, too, belongs to knowledge when we understand how it came about.

Each Knot preserves the filigree of its own inquiry: sources, intuitions, convergences, reversals. What we have had to leave behind along the way remains visible.

We submit our own writing to the most exacting test.

We disperse the mists of the present, knowing that we inhabit them.

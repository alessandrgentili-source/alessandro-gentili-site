Professional scope

Where a conversation makes sense

These are areas where I can connect a strategic question to practical work.

Strategic positioning and communication

Roles or projects that call for a clear position, credible messages and communication shaped by market and cultural context.

Editorial systems and knowledge architecture

Work that turns scattered research, expertise and content into a coherent structure people can navigate and use.

AI-supported workflows and operating systems

Practical uses of AI for research, synthesis and quality checks, with ownership and human judgment kept visible.

Cultural, civic and research-based projects

Collaborations that need careful reading of place, communities and public questions as well as sound execution.

Before writing

A useful first message

A short note is enough. Tell me the context, the role or project, and what needs to be solved or explored. It helps to know who is involved in the decision and what a useful next step might be.

That gives us a better starting point than a brief request without its setting.

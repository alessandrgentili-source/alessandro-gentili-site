Working method

From interpretation to execution

Cultural, economic, technological and market signals become decisions, messages and work with clear responsibility.

1. Read the context

Identify the forces shaping the market, the organization and the language around it.

2. Define the position

Choose a credible direction, the audiences it serves and the priorities it requires.

3. Build the architecture

Translate the position into message hierarchy, editorial paths and usable knowledge.

4. Create operating discipline

Assign ownership, set workflows and review points, and produce concrete outputs that can be checked and improved.

Context and judgment

Culture as analytical infrastructure

Political philosophy, sociology, economics, literature, geography and technology help me read change, recognize tensions and understand how language shapes reputation. They also reveal indirect effects of decisions that a narrow market view can miss. This wider reading informs practical choices.

Operating principle

AI and judgment

AI is an operating layer, not the author of judgment. It can help with research, comparison, synthesis, structure, revision and QA. Context, responsibility, taste, prioritization and final decisions remain human.

Alessandro Gentili · Marketing director and author

The work of judgment

On a floor plan, a home is a set of rooms. At six in the evening, a laptop, a schoolbook and the plates for dinner can all lay claim to the same table.

For more than twenty years I have worked as a marketing director and strategic consultant for premium international design brands, chiefly in kitchens, appliances and living spaces. The home is where an industry’s idea of how people live meets the arrangements people make for themselves.

Before a team settles on a design brief, someone has to decide whose account of life at home to trust. I write about the authority such decisions carry. In my work with AI, I design ways for people to examine the evidence behind them.

What a market leaves open

Suppose a family asks for a more flexible room. They may have less space, their working hours may have changed, or someone may need care. Each reading would give a design team a different brief. In marketing, I have to choose a reading I can defend and give the people designing, selling and communicating the work a direction they can use.

Writing gives me time to stay with what that decision leaves open. Who gets to describe a need? How does one person’s way of living become the model others are expected to follow? My essays explore what happens when those descriptions acquire authority.

In Cerchi d’inchiostro, I bring authors together around questions like these. Epicurus asks which desires are worth satisfying. Gabriel Tarde helps explain how a desire travels between people. Daniel Kahneman examines what happens when we judge under uncertainty. Reading them together makes it harder to write the word preference in a brief without asking how it was formed.

Who answers for the decision?

Consider a sentence I am about to publish. An AI system can suggest a connection between two authors. I need to be able to return to each text, see where the comparison breaks down and decide whether the sentence belongs under my name.

In The Separation of Cognitive Powers, I explore how to keep the proposal, the check and the authority to act distinct. The essay sets out the argument. An interactive presentation follows it through an editorial review. Whether that approach would work in another organization is a question to test there.

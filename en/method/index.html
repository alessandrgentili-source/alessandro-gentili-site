International · Method

How I read complexity and turn it into positioning, language and systems.

Marketing is not promotion. It is the discipline that connects society, technology, market, language and reputation. The work begins before the campaign: with interpretation, hierarchy and a decision about where it is credible to stand.

Principle

AI accelerates. Judgment governs.

AI can assist research, structure, revision and QA. It cannot carry responsibility for context, taste or final decisions.

Six passages

The method.

1. Read the context

Map cultural, economic, technological and market signals, including what is changing around the organization rather than only inside it.

2. Find the position

Identify what is credible, distinctive, durable and useful — not merely what is available to say.

3. Build the narrative architecture

Define message hierarchy, vocabulary, tone, formats and the relation between long-term identity and short-term communication.

4. Design the operating system

Turn strategy into ownership, editorial flows, review layers, knowledge bases, metrics and decision rhythms.

5. Use AI with judgment

Use models for research, comparison, synthesis and checks while keeping human review explicit at points of consequence.

6. Move into execution

Translate direction into pages, campaigns, presentations, workflows, publishing systems and measurable next actions.

Operating discipline

Reduce dispersion, not complexity.

The aim is not to make complex situations look simple. It is to make the relevant structure visible: priorities, ownership, dependencies, evidence and the moments where a decision must be made.

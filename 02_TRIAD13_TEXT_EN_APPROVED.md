# Knowledge, consequences and decision

**Question:** Who can afford to wait when acting may cause lasting harm, but delaying a decision carries a cost of its own?

Morin examines the limits of knowledge and the relationships an explanation may overlook. Carson follows the consequences of intervention through living systems, often far beyond the original decision. Keynes shows how uncertainty can hold back investment and employment, leaving others to bear the cost of waiting.

Together, they open a civic question: **who has the freedom to wait, and who must live with the consequences while others decide?**

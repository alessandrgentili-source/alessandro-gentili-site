## Shared relation

The Italian triad defines the threshold as:
- Marx — capital, labor, alienation;
- Nietzsche — nihilism, values, death of God;
- Leopardi — desire, infinity, modernity.

Its source description reads modernity as a wound through:
- the economic machine organizing labor;
- the crisis of values exposing the person to emptiness;
- the disproportion of desire before a finite world.

English editorial question:

> How does modernity organize what we produce, what we believe and what we desire?
